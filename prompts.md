# Prompts

Paste these into one Studio chat, in order. Replace anything in `[brackets]`.

---

## 1. Kick it off

Attach the `skill/make-app-reel` folder, then:

```
Install the attached make-app-reel skill in this workspace, then use it to make a short
vertical reel about my app [APP NAME], which I built in Studio.

Start by interviewing me. Treat my app on its own terms: don't copy any earlier reel's
script, look or music.
```

**Studio does:** installs the instructions, finds your app and the chat you built it in, and
asks you a few questions.
**You do:** answer them. You don't need to be a storyteller: give real details and moments,
and Studio shapes them into the story. The two that matter most:
- What's the most interesting thing about the app?
- What was the easiest or most fun part of building it?

---

## 2. Pick a tone, then a direction, and settle the script

Studio suggests a few tones that suit your app (serious, artsy, dry and funny...) with a
sample line for each. Pick one. Then it proposes a few story directions and a script.
Reply in your own words, for example:

```
Go with the [TONE] tone and option [N]. Make [LINE] sound more like me: [HOW YOU'D SAY IT].
Cut anything that makes building it sound like work.
```

Repeat until you'd say it out loud. Then: `Save the final script.`

---

## 3. Footage

```
Make the screen footage for the script. Made-up data only.
```

**You do:** look at the sample frames Studio shows. Check nothing private is visible.

---

## 4. Film your take

No prompt. Phone upright at eye level, window light on your face, one take straight to
camera, a second of silence at the start and end. Then attach it:

```
Here's my take. Edit the reel.
```

---

## 5. Watch it and ask for changes

Watch it with sound on, as a stranger scrolling would. Ask for specific changes, for example:

```
- The first two seconds don't hook me; open on [X] instead.
- Cut the part about [X], it makes it sound like work.
- The music feels [too busy / too sad]; make it [calmer / warmer].
```

Studio checks every change still keeps all your words whole and captions in one place.
